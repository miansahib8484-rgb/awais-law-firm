# Awais Law Associates — Trusted Legal Representation in Complex Commercial & Civil Matters

Awais Law Associates is a Lahore-based law firm led by Muhammad Awais Ahsan Joiya, Advocate High Court and Former Assistant Advocate General, Punjab. The firm represents businesses, financial institutions, and private clients across corporate, banking, civil, criminal, tax, family, intellectual property, arbitration, and overseas-Pakistani legal matters.

## Practice Areas

1. **Corporate & Commercial** — company registration, contracts, SECP compliance, mergers and disputes. [/practice-areas/corporate-commercial/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/corporate-commercial/)
2. **Banking & Financial Litigation** — recovery suits, loan defaults, cheque dishonour cases. [/practice-areas/banking-finance/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/banking-finance/)
3. **Civil & Property Litigation** — property disputes, recovery suits, adverse possession claims. [/practice-areas/civil-property/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/civil-property/)
4. **Constitutional Litigation** — writ petitions and fundamental rights cases before the Lahore High Court. [/practice-areas/constitutional-law/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/constitutional-law/)
5. **Criminal & NAB/FIA Defence** — NAB references, bail applications, FIA and cybercrime matters. [/practice-areas/criminal-nab-fia/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/criminal-nab-fia/)
6. **Revenue, Tax & Land Law** — FBR notices, mutation disputes, land revenue matters. [/practice-areas/revenue-tax-land/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/revenue-tax-land/)
7. **Trademark & IP** — trademark registration and intellectual property protection. [/practice-areas/trademark-ip/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/trademark-ip/)
8. **Overseas Pakistanis Legal Services** — property, inheritance, and succession matters managed remotely via power of attorney. [/practice-areas/overseas-pakistanis/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/overseas-pakistanis/)
9. **Arbitration** — domestic and international arbitration, including foreign award enforcement. [/practice-areas/arbitration/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/arbitration/)
10. **Family Law & Matrimonial Matters** — khula, divorce, child custody, and maintenance cases. [/practice-areas/family-law/](https://www.awaislawassociates.com/practice-areas/family-law/)
