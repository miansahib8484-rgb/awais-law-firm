Adverse Possession — The Standard Courts Actually Apply

Under the Limitation Act 1908, a person in adverse possession for 12 continuous years may, in certain circumstances, extinguish the original owner's right to sue for possession — though this is not automatically the same as acquiring formal, registered title. Lahore High Court decisions have held that a claim fails where the occupant has acknowledged the true owner's title, even informally, such as through a rent payment or a request for permission.

Recovery & Possession Suits

Recovery of possession, money recovery, and ejectment suits are among the most common civil matters the firm handles, typically requiring a documented paper trail — a lease, an acknowledgment, a payment record — to move quickly through the court system.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is adverse possession under Pakistani law?

Under the Limitation Act 1908, occupying property for 12 continuous years can, in certain circumstances, extinguish the original owner's right to sue for possession, though this differs from acquiring formal registered title.

How long does a civil recovery suit take in Lahore?

Timelines vary significantly with case complexity and court schedules; a well-documented recovery suit with clear evidence generally moves faster than a contested title dispute.

Can I get an injunction to stop construction or transfer of disputed property?

Yes, where the facts support it, an application for a temporary injunction or status-quo order can be filed to preserve the position while the underlying dispute is heard.

What documents matter most in a property dispute?

Registered title deeds, mutation records (fard/Jamabandi), prior sale agreements, and any correspondence acknowledging or disputing ownership are typically central to the case.

Do you handle boundary and encroachment disputes?

Yes, including encroachment removal, boundary wall disputes, and unlawful possession matters before revenue authorities and civil courts.

Can overseas Pakistanis pursue a civil property case remotely?

Yes, through a power of attorney, with the firm managing filings and appearances in Lahore on the client's behalf.
