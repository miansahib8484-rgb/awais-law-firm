Child Custody Lawyer in Lahore

Custody disputes rarely follow the assumptions people bring into them — the law does not hand custody to either parent by default. This page explains the standard Guardian Courts actually apply, and what a realistic custody or visitation case looks like in Lahore.

Why Parents Bring Custody Matters Here

Custody cases are handled personally by Advocate Muhammad Awais Ahsan Joiya, who treats these matters with the same evidence-first preparation he brings to litigation generally — because in a welfare-of-the-minor case, the party who can actually document the child's stability, schooling and daily routine is the one whose position the court can rely on.

Guardianship and custody petitions in Pakistan are filed under the Guardians and Wards Act 1890, and decided by the Guardian Court on what is called the welfare of the minor — not on which parent is the mother or the father. That surprises a lot of people, especially fathers who assume custody automatically goes to the mother, and mothers who assume the same in reverse. Neither assumption is correct in law, though in practice, for very young children, custody often does go to the mother under the concept of hizanat, subject to conditions.

What the court actually looks at is more practical than people expect: the child's age, which household offers more day-to-day stability, whether schooling would be disrupted, each parent's living situation and ability to care for the child, and — once a child is old enough to express a reasonable preference — what the child wants. None of these factors decide the case alone; the court weighs them together.

Custody and Visitation Are Usually Separate Questions

Losing primary custody does not mean losing access to your child. Visitation rights are normally addressed alongside custody, and we push to have both resolved in the same proceeding rather than leaving visitation to be fought over separately later, which only prolongs the dispute for everyone, especially the child.

When a Guardian Certificate Is Also Needed

If the child owns or stands to inherit property, a separate guardian certificate may be required to manage that property on the child's behalf, in addition to the custody order itself. This gets missed often, and it matters — banks and revenue authorities will not release funds or transfer property to a parent acting for a minor without one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the mother automatically get custody of young children in Pakistan?

Not automatically, though the concept of hizanat generally favours the mother for younger children, subject to conditions such as her not having remarried in a way that affects the child's welfare, among other factors. Courts still apply the welfare-of-the-minor test rather than a fixed rule.

At what age can a child choose which parent to live with?

There is no fixed statutory age; older children's preferences carry more weight, and the court exercises judgment on whether the child is mature enough to express a reasoned preference.

Can a father get custody in Pakistan?

Yes. Fathers regularly obtain custody, particularly for older children or where the welfare factors favour it. The law does not bar fathers from custody — it applies the same welfare standard to both parents.

What happens to custody if one parent moves abroad?

This is decided case by case, weighing the disruption to the child's schooling and routine against the benefits the parent argues for. It is one of the more contested issues in custody litigation and usually needs strong documentary evidence either way.

Can custody orders be changed later?

Yes, custody orders can be revisited if circumstances materially change — for example a change in either parent's living situation, remarriage, or evidence that the current arrangement is no longer serving the child's welfare.

Is a guardian certificate the same as a custody order?

No. A custody order decides who the child lives with. A guardian certificate is a separate document needed to manage the child's property or inheritance — you may need one, the other, or both depending on the situation.
