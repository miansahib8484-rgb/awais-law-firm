Frequently Asked Questions

How do I recover money through a recovery suit in Lahore?

As a recovery suit lawyer in Lahore, we file a money recovery suit before the civil or banking court depending on the nature of the debt, then pursue execution proceedings once a decree is granted. Supporting documents — agreements, cheques, or acknowledgment of debt — strengthen the claim significantly.

What is the difference between pre-arrest and post-arrest bail in NAB cases?

Pre-arrest bail is sought before formal arrest to prevent detention during a NAB inquiry, while post-arrest bail is filed after arrest has occurred. As a NAB bail lawyer in Lahore, we generally recommend applying for pre-arrest bail the moment a call-up notice is received. See our detailed guide on pre-arrest bail in NAB cases.

Can a foreign arbitration award be enforced in Pakistan?

Yes. Pakistan is a signatory to the New York Convention, and foreign arbitration award enforcement in Pakistan is handled through the Recognition and Enforcement (Arbitration Agreements and Foreign Arbitral Awards) Act 2011. Read our full breakdown on enforcing a foreign arbitration award in Pakistan.

What happens if a company director ignores an SECP compliance notice?

Ignoring SECP notices can expose directors to personal liability, penalties, and disqualification under the Companies Act 2017. As a director liability lawyer in Lahore, we advise prompt written response and, where needed, representation before SECP. Learn more in our article on directors' liabilities under the Companies Act 2017.

How do banks recover unpaid loans in Pakistan?

Banks typically initiate recovery through the Financial Institutions (Recovery of Finances) Ordinance before banking courts, followed by enforcement of mortgages or hypothecated assets. As a banking court recovery suit lawyer in Lahore, we represent both lenders and borrowers. See our analysis of the SBP enforcement framework and NPL litigation.

What is a mutation dispute and how is it resolved?

A mutation dispute arises when land revenue records are incorrectly transferred or updated. As a land record correction lawyer in Lahore, we file applications before revenue officers to correct fraudulent or erroneous mutation entries, and pursue appeals where relief is refused.

What is the khula procedure in Pakistan?

A wife seeking khula files a suit before the Family Court; if reconciliation efforts fail, the court can dissolve the marriage on khula even without the husband's consent, generally requiring her to return the dower received. As a family lawyer in Lahore, we prepare the khula papers, represent clients through the mandatory reconciliation stage, and handle the union council divorce certificate once the decree is final.
